OFFICIAL FIELD MANUAL // NiX COMMAND

Command made simple.

You don't steer individual soldiers. You are the commander. Each turn, choose one order. Your troops and the computer rival carry out their orders at the same time. Watch the result, then make your next decision.

⚔️
CHARGE

Strong forward assault. Especially effective against an opponent that is regrouping. Vulnerable to concentrated ranged fire.

🛡️
DEFEND

Greatly reduces damage. Helps your troops recover their footing and counters enemy charges. Deals a little return fire.

↗️
FLANK

Moves around the enemy's defenses. Very effective against Defend, but vulnerable when the enemy anticipates it with a Volley.

🎯
VOLLEY

Ranged attack from cover. Especially good against a Charge or Flank. Less effective against a well-prepared defense.

✚
REGROUP

Restore a few troops to your force. Excellent after taking losses, but risky if the opposing commander chooses Charge.

◈
FIELD SYMBOLS

Blue markers = your troops. Orange markers = rival troops. Health bars show soldiers remaining. Gold circles = defending. Green plus signs = recovered troops. Glowing trails = attacks.

How to win

Reduce the rival force to zero before it does the same to yours. You can take as much time as you like to pick an order. On Easy, you start with extra troops and the rival makes more forgiving choices.

Keyboard: 1 Charge · 2 Defend · 3 Flank · 4 Volley · 5 Regroup · Enter Execute · H Guide · Esc Close guide

The rival commander is simulated in your browser and does not connect to real players. Difficulty and battle decisions are calculated locally. Progress stores win/loss totals on this browser only.
