How the game works

Your genetics field guide

Answer questions to earn points. On interactive Punnett squares, choose an offspring genotype from the allele buttons, then tap a blank square to place it. Fill every square and press Check Answer.

This example crosses Bb × bb. Two of four combinations are Bb, and two are bb. A 1:1 genotypic ratio means 50% predicted for each genotype.

Accessibility: All answers and Punnett-square cells can be reached with Tab and activated using Enter or Space. Use the Reduce motion button to stop animations. Sound is optional. The game has a count-up timer for bonus points but no time limit.

  1. Read the built-in lesson at the start of each mission. Reopen it whenever you want.
  2. Open the concept guide above any question for a short, targeted explanation, without a score penalty.
  3. Choose your answer or fill in the genotype grid.
  4. Use a hint if needed, at a small point cost.
  5. Learn from each explanation — you can retry one time before the solution is revealed.
  6. Complete all 25 challenges to see your final result.
