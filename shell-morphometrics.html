How the estimate works

The tool checks mirror symmetry and elongation. A strongly asymmetric, elongated mesh suggests a gastropod. A near-symmetric, less elongated mesh suggests a bivalve. This is an exploratory geometry rule. Scans of single valves, damaged shells, and unusual shapes can be uncertain or misleading. Select a graph manually when you know the shell type.

    How to use the lab
    
    
      
  1. Choose a mesh and select “Analyze shell” for two views and a proportion chart. Open “Compare two shells” to add a moving specimen B.
    2. 
      
  2. For your own files, use triangulated OBJ, PLY, STL or self-contained GLB. Each file can contain up to 500,000 triangles and be up to 100 MB. Files are processed in your browser, and the analysis uses geometry only.
    3. 
      
  3. Choose original dimensions for scans with matching calibrated units. Use RMS surface radius to compare shape by rescaling B to A. The example pair uses this shape mode because its physical units have not been verified.
    4. 
      
  4. Select “Align specimens.” Switch viewer modes to inspect the fit and open “Measurements & export” for the numbers.
    5. 
    

  

  

    Alignment method and result interpretation
    
Iterative closest point (ICP) tests 24 starting orientations, refines the four strongest candidates and selects the fit with the lowest bidirectional squared surface distance. Blue marks smaller unsigned B-to-A distances and red marks distances at or above the displayed cap. Check the apex, aperture and siphonal canal before interpreting the fit.

    
Statistics are based on 5,000 area-distributed samples in each direction. RMS gives larger deviations more weight. P95 is the distance at or below which approximately 95% of the samples fall. The sampled maximum is the largest observed distance among those samples.

    
Distances use the reference mesh’s units in shape mode. A close fit does not establish species identity or anatomical correspondence. Validate registration and physical scale before research use.

  

  

    Manual landmark comparison
    
Enter corresponding XYZ landmarks in the same anatomical order. The first three points must be distinct and non-collinear. They define the alignment frame. Centroid-size normalization is optional.
