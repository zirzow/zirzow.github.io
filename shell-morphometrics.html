Landmark comparison workspace

Enter corresponding XYZ landmarks from calibrated scans in the same anatomical order. Use at least three non-collinear points. This prototype performs a three-point anatomical frame alignment, optionally normalized by centroid size, and reports landmark RMS deviation. It is not a full Procrustes fit or surface registration.

Research protocol
1. Acquire
Obtain original OBJ, PLY, STL or GLB files, accession identifiers, verified taxonomy and physical scale.
2. Landmark
Define repeatable landmarks at the apex, shoulder, aperture and siphonal canal. Use homologous points and document repeatability.
3. Register
Compare calibrated and size-normalized geometry; validate full surface registration in CloudCompare or SlicerMorph.
4. Evaluate
Use multiple specimens per species and size class, assess allometry and perform geometric morphometric statistics in R.
Proposed comparison
Florida horse conch (Triplofusus giganteus) and Prince horse conch (Triplofusus princeps) are candidate taxa. No species-level conclusions or measured results are presented here. Surface deviation, curvature and statistical morphospace analysis are future development goals.
