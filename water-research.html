Methods & limitations

Ordinary least squares on the earliest 75% of paired days, evaluated on the remaining 25%. Require at least 24 paired days, at least six withheld days, variable training rainfall, and nonconstant training response. Baseline predicts the training mean. Holdout R² uses the holdout mean; negative values are possible. The selected rainfall window is a user-defined hypothesis; comparing many windows or analytes is exploratory and can inflate apparent associations. Seasonality, tide, flow, sampling schedule, spatial rainfall differences, censoring and serial correlation are not controlled. No forecast uncertainty interval is estimated. Scenario estimates are limited to the observed training rainfall range and shown only if the model beats the holdout baseline on MAE.

Source: Florida DEP WIN · NOAA KFMY station. TKN is not total nitrogen; no TN is inferred. Agency laboratory metadata does not independently establish certification.
