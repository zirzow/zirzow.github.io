About these visualizations

Community and agency datasets are presented separately because sampling methods, quality controls, reporting intervals and measurement units may differ. Screening colors are not real-time swimming advisories. Agency-reported laboratory metadata is not independent confirmation of certification. Historical data may be incomplete or revised by its source.
