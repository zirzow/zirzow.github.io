Interactive sampling map

For non-bacterial parameters, colors represent relative quartiles of the latest eligible values in the selected view, not regulatory thresholds. For bacteria, colors use your existing screening bands for the latest eligible result of the selected indicator, not a current swimming safety determination. All monitoring locations mode uses teal for station coverage, not concentration. Historical bacteria results retain their screening colors with a dashed outline when older than 45 days; these are not current advisories. Gray means no eligible result for the selected parameter. Qualified or commented results remain available in the table. E. coli BAV is a freshwater screening value; site-specific applicability must be checked.

About the data

Data providers: Lee County Environmental Laboratories and City of Fort Myers via Florida DEP WIN. Results may be provisional. WIN qualifiers and original units are retained. Comments are flagged but not published. Verify laboratory accreditation, method, matrix, sampling conditions and regulatory applicability with the reporting agency before making a regulatory claim.
