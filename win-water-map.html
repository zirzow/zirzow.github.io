Public data transparency · sample collection dates

Monitoring network & data freshness

Default colors indicate the age of the latest reported sample at each location, not whether water is safe. Switch to a specific parameter to see measurement colors. Dates and counts reflect the loaded WIN feed, which may be incomplete.

For non-bacterial parameters, colors represent relative quartiles of the latest eligible values in the selected view, not regulatory thresholds. For bacteria, colors use your existing screening bands for the latest eligible result of the selected indicator, not a current swimming safety determination. Default freshness colors show the latest sample date across all parameters at each exact WIN location. In parameter mode, fill color shows the latest eligible reported value; dashed outlines distinguish historical data and a purple outline highlights observations older than 365 days. Gray means no eligible result for the selected parameter or no known date in freshness mode. Qualified or commented results remain available in the table. E. coli BAV is a freshwater screening value; site-specific applicability must be checked.

About the data

Data providers: Lee County Environmental Laboratories and City of Fort Myers via Florida DEP WIN. Results may be provisional. WIN qualifiers and original units are retained. Comments are flagged but not published. Verify laboratory accreditation, method, matrix, sampling conditions and regulatory applicability with the reporting agency before making a regulatory claim.
