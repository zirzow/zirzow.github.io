04 / Causes & communities close to my heart

What I believe in.

The work, waterways, and cycling community that continue to inspire me far beyond the studio.

A cause I care deeply about
🌊

Protecting the waters I love

Clean water matters to me not just as a scientist, but as someone who treasures Southwest Florida's wild places and the life they support. I am proud to volunteer with Calusa Waterkeeper and to support their work in water-quality monitoring, environmental education, and advocacy.

When you visit this site, you are also getting a window into the larger mission that helps guide so much of my teaching, outreach, and conservation work.

Learn about Calusa Waterkeeper ↗ Support clean water ↗
A cycling community I love

Riding farther together

Caloosa Riders Bicycle Club is a year-round Southwest Florida cycling community that brings riders together for fitness, friendship, adventure, and advocacy. I love being part of a group that helps riders grow while also championing cyclist safety and enjoyment on the road.

From beginner-friendly rides to endurance events and advocacy for safer cycling, the club reflects the sense of adventure and community that keeps me coming back to the bike.
