How to Use This Monitoring Page

Data are requested from the configured Calusa Waterkeeper ArcGIS endpoint when the page loads. Availability depends on public access to that dataset.

01. Toggle Historic Sites

Compare 45-Day Active vs. Past Locations

By default, the map highlights only sites sampled within the last 45 days. Click Historic Sites OFF / ON at the bottom right of the map (or use the button below) to display historically sampled collection sites as small grey points labeled with their site codes.

02. Explore Trends Over Time

Explore Indicator-Specific Results

Click any active or historic pin on the map—or choose a station from the dropdown in Site Trends Over Time below—to select an indicator and plot its historical samples against that indicator’s EPA Beach Action Value reference line.
