Contact signup privacy

Your email address and consent are collected to keep in touch. Name, phone, mailing address and interests are optional. No automated emails are sent and no email confirmation is required.

Contact records are stored privately in Google Sheets through Google Apps Script. Your information is not published in the website repository or sold. Optional phone and address details do not authorize calls, texts or postal marketing.

To withdraw consent or request correction or deletion, use the contact withdrawal option on the signup service when available. You can follow the public RSS feed without providing any contact information.
